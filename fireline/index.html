CREW INDUCTION / TOUCH CONTROLS

Make the correction.

  1. 01Load deliberately.Select HE or SMOKE. Swipe the LOAD button upward, or tap it. Wait for the shell to seat.
  2. 02Lay the weapon.Tap ASSIST in the bottom dock. With AID ON, hold the suggested correction buttons until they stop. The white NEXT ROUND ring turns green when aligned. For moving targets, align with the blue ARRIVAL diamond and fire while green. Manual controls: drag the AIM pad or tap/hold its arrows. ↑ elevation brings impacts closer; ↓ sends them further. FINE reduces sensitivity.
  3. 03Fire, then observe.Tap FIRE. By default the observer optics open automatically. AIM is selected by default: dragging the scene adjusts the mortar. Select LOOK to move the optics only. Tap OPTICS for ± zoom, FIND and AIM / LOOK. Open ASSIST and tap the vehicle name for the next target; TRACK follows it with the camera. Dragging in LOOK turns tracking off. The pad and arrows always adjust the gun.
  4. 04Read the miss.Dust, craters, and the LAST IMPACT ring show where your round landed. SHORT: lower elevation. LONG: raise it. BEHIND MOVING TARGET means it travelled past your impact. Recheck the arrival estimate before each round.
  5. 05Keep the line alive.HE damages targets. SMOKE slows targets within its screen for 20 seconds. FOCUS runs flight, target movement and deadlines at half speed while aiming stays responsive. In the final mission, firing raises exposure. RELOCATE when an incoming strike is announced.

The white NEXT ROUND marker predicts the next landing point; green means good aim. The blue ARRIVAL diamond estimates a moving target’s position for that flight time. It updates for turns, smoke and suppression; another explosion can change movement after you fire. The central optic reticle is a viewing reference. LAST records the previous shot; numbered craters show actual impacts. ASSIST folds away without disabling guidance. AID OFF inside that panel restores manual aiming. Practice and movement assistance have separate preferences.
