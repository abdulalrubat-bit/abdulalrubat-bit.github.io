About

Before making games I spent years in warehousing and logistics — pick and pack, receiving, dispatch, forklifts, inventory systems. That experience runs through the work rather than sitting on top of it.

Everything here is built small and self-contained: hand-written, no engine where none is needed, fully playable offline, with no accounts, no sign-in and no data collection. Where there is advertising, it is opt-in only — you choose to watch something in exchange for a benefit, and the game is complete without ever doing so.

Games

Warehouse Empire

Idle logistics  ·  Android  ·  Free

Start on the floor picking orders by hand and build up to a fully automated distribution empire. Eight equipment tiers unlock in the order you would actually meet them — hand picking, pallet trolley, counterbalance forklift, reach truck, conveyor line, sorting robot, automated crane, distribution hub. Hire managers, fill contracts, and climb the career ladder. Keeps earning while you are away.

Ultimate Survivor

Arena survival  ·  Android  ·  Free

Hold out against escalating waves in a top-down arena shooter. Build a loadout from cards between rounds and push as deep as the difficulty curve lets you.
